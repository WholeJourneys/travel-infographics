Slovenia: Julian Alps and Soča Valley

Eleven nights in four comfortable bases, with optional extra nights, June 16 to 27, 2027: Ljubljana, Lake Bohinj, Kranjska Gora and Bovec. Each base has a day of walking from the door or a short transfer, with guided days for the bigger mountains, and a free day in Bovec. Where a day has two options, you choose. Everything here is a suggestion, and the hiking and driving times are approximate until the hotels and routes are confirmed.
